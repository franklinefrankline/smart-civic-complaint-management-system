{{ tr('Complaint Resolution Workflow') }}

{{ tr('A transparent, four-tier coordination between citizens, officers, technicians, and leadership.') }}

1

{{ tr('Citizen Registration & Address Details') }}

The citizen registers or signs in, fills out the grievance form with title, category, description, and municipal ward and street address. Duplicate detection prevents redundant reports in the same zone.

2

{{ tr('Department Officer Acceptance & Worker Assignment') }}

The complaint enters the designated department queue. The officer inspects the photo, approves the priority level, and assigns an available field technician with specific execution instructions.

3

{{ tr('Field Technician On-Site Execution') }}

The field worker accepts the assignment, updates status to "In Progress", uploads pre-work condition photos, executes necessary repairs, and submits completion notes accompanied by after-work photos.

4

{{ tr('Officer Quality Verification & Final Resolution') }}

The officer verifies the submitted evidence. If work is satisfactory, the officer approves resolution. If incomplete, it is returned for rework. Upon resolution, the citizen receives an alert and provides star ratings.
