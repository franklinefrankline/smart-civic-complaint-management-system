About the Smart Civic Platform

An enterprise municipal grievance resolution ecosystem bridging citizens, department heads, and field crews.

Our Civic Mission

Municipal governance succeeds when citizens have an accessible, transparent, and responsive channel to report civic disruptions. The Smart Civic Complaint Management and Tracking System replaces traditional paper-based or opaque complaint desks with end-to-end digital tracking.

Every complaint is digitally timestamped, tracked with administrative ward precision, bound to rigorous SLA resolution benchmarks, and validated with before-and-after photographic evidence prior to case closure.

Multilingual Access

Available in English, Tamil, and Hindi to ensure every citizen can report issues comfortably in their preferred language.

SLA & Escalation

Automated time-tracking flags overdue tasks and escalates unattended grievances directly to administrative oversight.

Verified Proofs

Field technicians must upload photographic evidence before and after executing repairs, verified by department officers.
